# Personal Staffer

Personal Staffer is an installed Windows and Android job discovery and application tracker for one explicitly authorized Google account. The server continues discovery while devices are asleep. User hardware is an Asus Vivobook Pro 15 and Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. The full contract is docs/BUILD_SPECIFICATION.md.

The daily workflow is to inspect evidence-backed U.S. jobs, open the original application destination, save or explicitly record an application, and follow the permanent application timeline. No resume, automated applications, LinkedIn login or messages are part of this product.

Main destinations, in order: Notifications, Homepage, People, Saved Jobs, Applied Jobs. Homepage tabs: Job Feed and Application Dashboard. Settings is secondary. Priority opportunities are additional to the regular daily quota. Saved and applied records retain snapshots despite closure or age. Uncertain claims remain labeled uncertain. Offline changes remain Pending until server acknowledgment; stale corrections require deliberate resolution.
